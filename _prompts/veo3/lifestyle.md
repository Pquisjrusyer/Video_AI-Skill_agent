# Veo 3 Prompt Library — Lifestyle Scene

> **Thư viện ý tưởng / tham khảo — không phải deliverable.** Output chuẩn ở `/assets` lượt 4C là thẻ `_prompts/veo3/video-prompt-card.md` (Veo 3.1 image-to-video từ first frame đã duyệt ở 4B, render 4/6/8s; độ dài render ≠ độ dài dùng). Người dùng tự render. Nếu file này mâu thuẫn với thẻ → theo thẻ.
>
> Mỗi prompt dưới đây là **image-to-video từ first frame đã duyệt**: phần mô tả bối cảnh/đạo cụ/ánh sáng dùng để viết prompt ảnh first frame (4A); trong prompt video chỉ giữ chuyển động + camera và mở bằng "Maintain the subject and scene from the first frame." 1 scene = 1 clip, tối đa 8s.

> **Khi dùng:** Tạo video lifestyle không có talking head — cảnh aspirational, product in-context, mood-setting.  
> **Best for:** Cold audience awareness, brand vibe, B-roll, Stories.

---

## Base Template

```
[SETTING DESCRIPTION — chi tiết môi trường]

[TIME OF DAY + LIGHTING: e.g., Late morning, warm golden light streaming from left window]
[COLOR PALETTE: e.g., Warm neutrals — cream, sage, warm white]
[MOOD: e.g., Calm, unhurried, aspirational but relatable — not luxury]

[ACTION/MOTION: 
- Describe exactly what happens, beat by beat
- Who/what moves, in which direction, how fast
]

[PRODUCT PLACEMENT: e.g., Product visible on counter — label facing camera, always in sharp focus]
[CAMERA: e.g., Slow push-in, handheld feel with micro-movement]
Audio: No dialogue, no music, no sound effects; quiet natural ambience only.

Format: [9:16 / 4:5 / 1:1]
Duration: [4s / 6s / 8s]
```

## Variation 1 — Morning Ritual Scene

```
A sun-filled bathroom, morning light from a frosted window. 
Color palette: White, warm cream, soft sage green.
On the marble counter: [PRODUCT] positioned center-right. 
A small ceramic dish with a face roller. Folded white towel.

A woman's hands (no face) enter from the right.
She picks up the product unhurriedly, dispenses one drop onto her fingertip.
Her forearm visible — she presses it gently to her cheek (off-camera).
She sets the product back down, slightly angled. Exhales — visible relaxation in her shoulders.

Camera: Starts medium, slowly drifts in. Product always in field of view.
Focus: Sharp on product. Hands in focus during motion. Background soft.
Audio: No dialogue, no music, no sound effects; quiet natural ambience only.

Format: 9:16 | Duration: 8s
```

> **Best for:** Skincare, wellness product — morning routine angle.

## Variation 2 — Outdoor/Lifestyle Scene

```
A sun-dappled backyard patio, late afternoon. 
Wooden table with a [PRODUCT] placed naturally in center. 
Nearby: a glass of water with ice, a book face-down, reading glasses.
Soft wind causes very slight leaf movement in background.

No person. Product alone in scene. Camera begins pulled back, seeing full table.
Slow push-in over the first 5 seconds — ending on close-up of product label.
At second 5: a hand enters from bottom frame, picks up product casually and pulls out of frame.

Lighting: Warm afternoon backlight — slight lens flare, golden hue.
Color: Warm wood tones, green foliage, amber product.
Audio: No dialogue, no music, no sound effects; quiet natural ambience only.

Format: 4:5 | Duration: 8s
```

> **Best for:** Active lifestyle brand, supplement, outdoor/wellness product.

## Variation 3 — Unboxing / Arrival Moment

```
Clean white kitchen table, bright overhead natural light.
A kraft paper box with [BRAND NAME] on label sits slightly off-center.
[PRODUCT] is resting against the box — as if just unboxed.

Slow sequence:
- (0–2s): Static shot — product and box, clean composition
- (2–4s): Slow push-in — focus moves to product label
- (4–8s): Hands enter, pick up product. Rotate to show back label briefly.

Lighting: Bright, clean overhead — minimal shadows. Feels like morning.
Audio: No dialogue, no music, no sound effects; quiet natural ambience only.

Format: 1:1 | Duration: 8s
```

> **Best for:** New customer retargeting, product launch, "this just arrived" UGC feel.

---

## Tips — Lifestyle Video

- **Product placement:** Luôn describe product position cụ thể trong frame — "center-right", "foreground, label facing camera"
- **Props:** Ít hơn là nhiều hơn. 2–3 contextual props là đủ. Quá nhiều props → cheap/cluttered
- **Hands vs no hands:** Lifestyle không có hands → abstract/editorial feel. Có hands → grounded, relatable. Chọn theo campaign tone.
- **Audio:** dùng dòng chuẩn "No dialogue, no music, no sound effects; quiet natural ambience only." — âm thanh clip bị tắt khi dựng, trừ khi audio-plan đánh dấu "tạo cùng clip".
