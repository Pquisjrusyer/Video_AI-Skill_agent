## Pain Points (ngôn ngữ khách hàng thực sự dùng)

### Pain Point 1 — Lipstick làm khô môi (lý do switch brand #1)

- Họ nói: *"every lipstick I try leaves my lips so dry"*, *"finally a lipstick that doesn't dry out my lips"*, *"I've tried so many and they all dry out my lips"*
- Underlying emotion: Frustrated — đã bị burned nhiều lần, mất trust vào category
- Trigger trong ad: "Still dealing with dry lips after wearing lipstick?"

### Pain Point 2 — Màu nhạt / sai màu so với ảnh

- Họ nói: *"the color actually looks like the photo"*, *"so pigmented, just like in pictures"*, *"not sheer like most lip products"*
- Underlying emotion: Disappointment từ experience cũ, sợ lãng phí tiền
- Trigger trong ad: "Tired of colors that look nothing like the swatch?"

### Pain Point 3 — Không tìm được Halal-certified makeup đáng tin ở Mỹ

- Họ nói: *"third-party certified halal"*, *"need to verify before buying"*, *"self-certified is not enough"*
- Underlying emotion: Skeptical, áp lực phải verify thêm bước
- Trigger trong ad: "Third-party certified halal — not just self-claimed."

### Pain Point 4 — Lip liner thường khô và khó dùng

- Họ nói: *"NOT DRY"*, *"so smooth"*, *"doesn't drag or pull"*, *"glides on easily"*
- Underlying emotion: Frustration với category mặc định khô
- Trigger trong ad: "Liner that glides, not drags."

## Desired Outcomes (điều họ thực sự muốn)

1. *"I want lips that look good AND feel comfortable all day — not dry, not sticky"*
2. *"I want makeup I can trust is actually halal/kosher — with proof, not just a claim"*
3. *"I want color that looks like the photos, not washed out when I put it on"*
4. *"I want an everyday lip product I can reach for without thinking twice"*

## Vocabulary — Từ khách hàng Kreizi hay dùng

### Từ/cụm từ CÓ dùng trong script (từ actual reviews)
```
Lips:
- "creamy", "moisturizing", "hydrating", "nourishing"
- "not drying", "NOT DRY", "doesn't dry out"
- "smooth", "soft", "comfortable all day"
- "not sticky", "lightweight", "glides on"
- "pigmented", "so pigmented", "color payoff"
- "true to color", "looks just like the photo"
- "lasts 4-6 hours", "doesn't fade as fast"
- "my lips but better" (MLBB)
- "everyday lip", "reach for it every day"

Certification / Trust:
- "third-party certified"
- "actually halal", "actually vegan"
- "verified clean ingredients"
- "peace of mind"
- "great quality for the price"

Switching language:
- "finally found one that doesn't dry out my lips"
- "switched from [X] because it was too drying"
- "haven't gone back since"
```

### Từ/cụm từ KHÔNG dùng (too corporate / unnatural)
```
- "innovative formula"
- "clinically proven" (chưa có nghiên cứu đăng ký)
- "revolutionary", "game-changing"
- "transform your lips"
- "luxury lip experience"
- "scientifically formulated"
```
